# 001-1m Which questions reach forward

`Slope_Credit_Scenario_Research_and_Design_Kit/research/recent_cases/akoustis/design/QUESTIONS_20240514.md` §1 now has
a rule, "What a question is conditioned on": a question is defined by the state on its own date; the answer to a
decision dated later on the path is never part of its event, stage, interval, class or facts; stated intentions about
later decisions are evidence; an interval ends on a date or a change of state, and a recurring decision is asked
again in the new state.

The first case found: the settlement offer opening on 22 May (day 8 on draw 0) is labelled stage I1 or I2 by whether
post-trial motions are filed, a decision dated 19 Jun (`tools/diagnose_chronological_start.py`). The stages, intervals
and contexts in the walk (settlement I0/I1/I2/I4, enforcement before and after the ruling, the response at entry,
ripe and post, the notes' routes, listing and distress occasions, and the class fields) were named by the order of the
hand-written walk, so more may reach forward the same way.

Go through every question node, read in the code, and set it against the rule: for each, what defines its stage or
interval, its class and its facts, and whether any of it can come from a decision dated later on some path. For each
that does, say in one or two plain sentences what the question asks on its own date instead, and what in the code
produces the forward reach. Read the code; run nothing heavy. Write the table to `var/diag/001-1m/inventory.md` and
give its findings in your final message. Change no product code.
