# 001-3c Deferred facts stop at the question's date; settlement recurs when the state changes

`QUESTIONS_20240514.md` §1 (kit, Akoustis design folder): a question is defined by the state on its own date; a later
decision's answer is never part of it; a recurring decision is asked again when its state materially changes.

Two things remain after the post-entry legal state was made to drive what is pending:

1. Some questions record their facts late, from the completed path (`Forecaster.record_late`, the deferred classes,
   the offering's close question `offering_closes`). The no-future check
   (`uv run python -m tools.check_question_futures --walker current --row 0`, a few minutes) finds the closing
   question of an offering started at entry (22 May) taking facts from the listing answer of 20 Oct (175 times in
   three minutes on draw 0). A deferred record must describe the state on the question's own date, with every later
   step left out, wherever the facts are read.
2. Settlement is asked in hand-written stages. It must be asked again when its state materially changes: motions filed,
   ruling, appeal, an approved stay taking effect (an early effective stay currently opens no settlement until the
   ruling), enforcement. The offer's formula and the acceptance question stay as they are.

Done when the no-future check reports neither on one draw for a few minutes, the no-skip check stays clean, and the
focused tests pass. Keep the four review-date draws in `run.txt`. No cloud and no judgment calls.
