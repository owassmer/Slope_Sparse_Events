# 001-13f: are the walk's question facts really dated, or is the checker wrong?

Outcome 001 requires, before the judgment model answers anything: every question's facts are as they stood before its
own answer. A question is defined by the state on its own date; later answers never define it. A question whose facts
carry anything from after its date is future-information leakage, a critical error: the judgment model would answer
from facts it could not have had, and the walked tree (about 6 million histories so far, all walked on the current walk
code) would have to be walked again.

Task 001-13e moved the dated-facts and no-skip checkers into `app/analysis/walk_checks.py` and ran them on every
history of one local piece, `var/pool-continuation` (written 8 October; which code wrote it is not known). All 8
histories it measured failed dated facts and none failed no-skip. One example (`var/diag/001-13e/timing-no-witness.json`):
draw 484, the appeal question `dispute_002:appeal|award1695000300|...` dated 24 July 2024 (day 71), kind
`dated_facts_mismatch`, changed `facts`, listing as later steps a judgment response and a cash-floor decision on
18 August 2024. Whether this is a real leak in the walk or a fault in the check (or a mismatch between an old piece and
the current code) is not known. A trial on real current pieces could not tell either: the exhaustive check did not
finish one batch in 20 minutes.

Real pieces from the current walk are now local: `var/remote/walk-f34/heavy-0/walk-f34-*/events.pkl.gz`, with their
manifests in `var/remote/walk-f34/out-0/var/walk-population/walk-f34-*/piece.json` (4 pieces, about 1,450 histories,
written by the walk code on this branch). Find out, on enough of their histories to be sure, whether the walk records
questions whose facts include anything from after their date. For each kind of mismatch found, say exactly which facts
differ, what values the recorded question had and what the dated state had, and whether the recorded value could
only be known later. Then:
- if the checker is wrong, fix the checker, with a test from a real history that shows the false alarm gone and a
  test showing a genuinely planted future fact still caught;
- if the walk leaks, do not fix the walk in this task; report the cause in the code, which questions and how many
  histories it touches, and the smallest real history that shows it.

Keep each measurement bounded and write results as they come; no command over a few minutes on this machine.
