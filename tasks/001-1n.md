# 001-1n A check that no question carries its future

`QUESTIONS_20240514.md` §1 (in the kit's Akoustis design folder) now says a question is defined by the state on its
own date, and the answer to a decision dated later on the path is never part of its event, stage, interval, class or
facts.

Write that as a check on any walk's emitted histories, beside the no-skip check in `tests/walk_order.py`: for every
question a history asks, rebuild its key, class and facts from the path replayed up to its own date with every
later-dated step removed, and compare with what the walk recorded. A difference is a violation, reported with the
question, its date and the later step it depended on.

Run it locally on one draw over the current walker's histories from the review date, where the whole tree is walked
(the settlement offer of 22 May, labelled by the 19 Jun motions answer, `tools/diagnose_chronological_start.py`, must
show up), and over the chronological walk's histories on one saved root. Report the violations by question node; do
not fix the walk or the questions. Put the 512-draw runs in `run.txt`. Data goes in `var/`. No cloud and no judgment
calls.
