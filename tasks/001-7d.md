# 001-7d Draws walk together, apart only where they must

The chronological walk can carry a population of draws and split it only where the draws' decision orders differ.
Walking draws together cost 2.4x less per history-draw than one at a time at 8 draws, and 5.2x less at 64, on
different prefixes (`var/diag/001-7a/report.md`). Finishing a history is now about 7.5x cheaper than it was
(`var/diag/001-7c/report.md`), so walking itself is a larger share of the time.

Make the production way of walking the tree carry all 512 draws from the review date together, splitting a population
only where its draws must go separately, and measure: the cost per history-draw against walking draws alone on the
same draws, at 8, 64 and 512 draws, for a few minutes each. Check that walking together gives the same histories,
cash and question records per draw as walking alone, and that both checks stay clean. Then put in `run.txt` what we
need for the full tree: the walk split into pieces that each finish within 20 minutes on a runner (split by the tree's
top decisions, or by draws, whichever the measurements favour), writing as they go. No cloud and no judgment calls.
