# 001-3a After entry, what is pending comes from the state

`QUESTIONS_20240514.md` §1 (kit, Akoustis design folder): a question is defined by the state on its own date; a later
decision's answer is never part of it; intervals end on dates or changes of state; recurring decisions are asked again
when the state changes.

The no-future check (`uv run python -m tools.check_question_futures --walker current --row 0`; four 20-minute draws
are summarised in `var/remote/001-1p/`) finds the post-trial motions answer (dated at its deadline, 28 days after entry)
defining questions dated before it. The settlement offer and acceptance of 22 May are labelled I1 or I2 by whether
motions are filed on 19 Jun. An offering started at entry has its closing framed the same way. On the no-motions
route, the engine moves the "final" date back to entry (`Chain.step` post_trial_motions, `F`), so the appeal and
enforcement after final judgment are framed as final at entry. `var/diag/001-1m/inventory.md` names the code
(`_Walk.motions`, `Chain.settlement_window`, `Chain.step`, `trigger_days`).

Make the post-entry legal state drive what is pending. After entry: motions can be filed until their deadline; the
time to appeal runs from entry, or from the ruling if motions are filed (FRAP 4(a)(4)); the automatic stay lasts 30
days (Rule 62(a)); enforcement becomes available as the law allows; settlement is a recurring decision asked in the
state of its own date and again when that state changes (motions filed, ruling, appeal, stay, enforcement). "No
motions" finalises the judgment on the motions deadline's passing, not retroactively at entry. Both walkers build
their questions from this. Cash booking rules and amounts stay as they are.

Done when the no-future check reports none of these reaches on one draw for a few minutes, and the no-skip check stays
clean. Put four review-date draws in `run.txt` (the existing lines). No cloud and no judgment calls.
