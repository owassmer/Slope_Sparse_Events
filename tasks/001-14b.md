# 001-14b: make the every-history dated-facts and no-skip checks fast enough for the whole tree

Outcome 001 requires, before the judgment model answers anything, that every question's facts are as they stood
before its own answer, and that the walk never skips a pending decision. Those two checks now run on every history
inside the pool's reduction stage (`app/analysis/walk_checks.py`, `app/analysis/tree_history_checks.py`, called from
`app/analysis/tree_parts.py`). They are the only thing that found a real future-information leak in the walk (task
001-13f), so they must run on all of it, not a sample.

They are far too slow. Measured by 001-13e: about 3.9 s per history against 0.07 s for the rest of the reduction;
about 54 hours for 8 million histories at 40 runner lines of 4 cores. On GitHub, one line given 4 real walk-f34 pieces
(1,446 histories) did not finish its first batch in 20 minutes. The profile (`var/diag/001-13e/profile`) shows one
failing history rebuilding the FutureChecker 619 times, dominated by event replay and ATM rebooking. The pool's
reduction stage must stay within about two hours of wall time for 8 million histories (each line a 4-core runner,
20 minutes at most, 40 at once), so the checks need to come to roughly 0.1 s per history or less.

Make them that fast without weakening them: every history, every draw it carries, the same verdicts. Histories in a
piece share long prefixes, and a question's before-answer state is the state the walk already passed through; a
check that rebuilds each history from scratch repeats that work. Whatever you do, the checks must still catch: the
real leak 001-13f found (fixture `tests/fixtures/walk_f34_walk-f34-0002_168_judgment_response.json`, which still leaks on
this code; it is a saved record of the old walk and stays leaking whatever the engine does), a planted future fact and a planted skipped decision; and must not raise the false alarms 001-13f cleared
(its other four fixtures). Show the verdicts are identical to the slow checker's on a few hundred real histories.

Real pieces are local: `var/remote/walk-f34/heavy-0/walk-f34-*/events.pkl.gz` with manifests in
`var/remote/walk-f34/out-0/var/walk-population/walk-f34-*/piece.json`. Keep each measurement bounded; anything longer
than a few minutes goes in `run.txt`. Another worker is changing `app/analysis/events.py` to remove that leak at the
same time; do not change the walk or the engine.

Done: on real pieces, the checks run on every history at a measured rate that projects within the two-hour reduction
budget for 8 million histories; identical verdicts to the slow checker on a few hundred real histories; the five
fixtures and the planted cases behave as above.
