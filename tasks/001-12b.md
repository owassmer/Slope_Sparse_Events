# 001-12b The baby-shelf limit after the annual update (Law)

The model lets the company sell shares on its shelf (underwritten offerings, N1, and at-the-market sales) up to its
authorized share capacity (`QUESTIONS_20240514.md` §2.6, kit Akoustis design folder). Research into the record
(`var/diag/001-12b/001-12a-report.md`) found the shelf was used under Form S-3 General Instruction I.B.1 as of 14 May
2024, with about $102M of shelf and $48M of at-the-market capacity disclosed; the January 2024 underwriting agreement
§3(a)(xxxiv) establishes I.B.1.

The SEC staff's guidance in force in 2024 (`var/diag/001-12b/sec-cdi-form-s3.txt`; use 114.02, 116.22, 116.23, and do
NOT apply 116.26, issued in 2026) is that eligibility is re-determined at each Section 10(a)(3) update, the annual
report on Form 10-K. A company whose public float is then below $75 million may continue on Form S-3 only under
General Instruction I.B.6: in any 12 months it may sell no more than one third of its public float, counting only
securities sold under I.B.6, with capacity measured immediately before each takedown and only securities actually
sold counted. Akoustis's fiscal year ends 30 June, so its FY2024 10-K falls inside the horizon.

Model it as Law: until the FY2024 10-K filing date (a scenario date from the company's filing record before the
cutoff, with its basis stated), I.B.1 as today; from that date, on each path, the public float at that date (shares held
by non-affiliates at the path's share price, the instruction's measure; take non-affiliate shares from the record and
state how) decides whether I.B.6 applies, and if it does, offerings and at-the-market sales are limited by the
remaining one-third capacity at each takedown. An offering whose amount exceeds the capacity left is not available,
and the question's situation says so (as it does for other unavailable offerings). Add the rule to §2.6 of
`QUESTIONS_20240514.md` with its basis (Law, Record) and update the kit manifest hash. Show its effect: on the saved
root and a few minutes from the review date, which paths lose an offering or at-the-market sales and by how much cash;
both checks clean; focused tests pass. No cloud and no judgment calls.
