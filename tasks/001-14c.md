# 001-14c: check a history's draws together, as the walk walks them

Outcome 001 requires, before the judgment model answers anything, that every question's facts are as they stood
before its own answer and that no pending decision is skipped, on every history of the walked tree. These two checks
(`app/analysis/walk_checks.py`, `app/analysis/tree_history_checks.py`, run inside the pool's reduction in
`app/analysis/tree_parts.py`) are what found a real future-information leak (task 001-13f); they must cover every
history and every draw it carries.

Task 001-14b made them about five times faster by reusing replay prefixes (0.47 s a history on 20 real histories, 45
draws; identical verdicts to the uncached checker), but the cost still grows with the number of draws a history
carries, because each draw is replayed on its own: a 100-history run stalled at a history carrying 82 draws, where
earlier ones carried one to five. The walk itself steps all 512 draws together as arrays (`app/analysis/events.py`,
`Chain`, vectorised over draws), and a history's draws share its steps; replaying them one at a time throws that away.
At about 0.2 s per history-draw and roughly 100 million history-draws in the tree, the checks would take some 36 hours
of wall time on 40 runner lines of 4 cores, longer than the reduction can be.

Replay each history once for all the draws it carries, in the engine's array form, and check every draw's facts and
order from that one replay. The checks stay independent of the walk's own recording: they rebuild the before-answer
state from the history's steps through the plain engine, as now; only the per-draw repetition goes. The target is the
whole reduction of about 8 million histories (about 100 million history-draws) within about six hours of wall time
on 40 lines of 4 cores, i.e. on the order of 0.03 s per history-draw or better, measured on real pieces including wide
histories. Six hours because the pool is not run once: any fix to the walk means walking and pooling again, and the
checks should stay a small part of that cycle (the walk alone takes days of waves).

What must hold: every history and every draw checked; the same verdicts as the uncached per-draw checker (show it on
a few hundred real histories including several carrying dozens of draws); the saved leaking fixture
(`tests/fixtures/walk_f34_walk-f34-0002_168_judgment_response.json`) still fails, the four cleared false alarms still
pass, a planted future fact and a planted skipped decision still fail. Real pieces are local in
`var/remote/walk-f34/heavy-0/` (manifests in `var/remote/walk-f34/out-0/var/walk-population/`) and in
`var/diag/001-13e/trial.sqlite`; `tools/benchmark_history_checks.py` measures and compares, flushing as it goes. Keep
local runs to a few minutes; longer ones go in `run.txt`. Do not change the walk or the engine's cash.

Done: the measured rate on real pieces, wide histories included, projects within the six-hour budget; identical
verdicts on a few hundred real histories; the fixtures and planted cases behave as above.
