# 001-5d Do histories with the same cash state share a future?

A measurement of the chronological walk (`var/diag/001-5a/measure_walk_sharing.md`) found that deep in the tree many
histories share their dated cash and obligations (for example 25 histories, 5 cash states) but differ in other state
it kept: pending decisions, walk flags such as the last response, the class and late records, and recorded answer
facts. Since then, questions are built from the dated state alone, so walk bookkeeping should no longer change what
happens next.

Test it empirically on one draw from the review date: at a few depths, group histories by their dated cash,
obligations and pending decisions (with their dates), walk each history's future, and compare the futures (each
step's cash and obligations, and each question's key and facts). Report how many groups have identical futures and,
where they differ, exactly which state field made them differ. That field belongs in the state; the rest do not. Keep
it to a few minutes locally; put larger samples in `run.txt`, each within 20 minutes. Results in `var/` and your
final message. Measure only. No cloud and no judgment calls.
