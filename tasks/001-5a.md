# 001-5a How much the walk could share, and where its time goes

The chronological walk now starts from the review date, asks the earliest pending decision on each draw, and its
checks hold. It lists every history: about 350 in three minutes on one draw, and the full tree is 512 draws. The plan
for speed is to walk each distinct state once and share its future: histories that reach the same dated state (the
same cash and obligations, the same pending decisions and dates, and the same facts later questions read) have the
same future.

Before anything is built, measure whether that pays, on one draw from the review date for a few minutes, and on one
or two native roots:
- At each decision depth, how many histories reach it and how many distinct states they are in. The state is
  everything that decides the future, including the facts and classes later questions read. Report also how many
  distinct states there would be on cash and obligations alone, to show what the question facts cost in sharing.
- Where the walk's time goes: building and replaying chains, reading dates for the frontier, building questions,
  booking, the checks.

Report the numbers and what they imply for a shared walk on all 512 draws (one machine and GitHub's runners), in
your final message, with the data in `var/`. Measure only; change no product behaviour. No cloud and no judgment calls.
