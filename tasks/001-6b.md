# 001-6b Which decisions make a draw's tree large

Knuth estimates of one draw's tree from the review date (`slope estimate-walk`, results under `var/remote/001-5c/`)
range from about 8,000 histories to about 476,000, and almost all of it is under "no settlement before the verdict".
The heavy probes are long chains of 2- to 4-way decisions; the probe records the branching at each level but not
which decision it was.

Attribute the size: with the same estimator, record each probe's decision at each level (node, context, how many
answers), and report how much of the estimated tree and walk time comes from each kind of decision and from how many
times it recurs on a path (the floor decision, offerings and their closing, settlement re-asked, responses, the notes,
listing, the court). Name the few decisions, or recurrences, that account for most of the size. Put the heavy draws
(146, 365, 438) and two light ones in `run.txt`, each within 20 minutes. Results in `var/` and your final message.
Measure only. No cloud and no judgment calls.
