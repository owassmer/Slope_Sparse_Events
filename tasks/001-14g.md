# 001-14g: size checked reduction lines from the measured rate, and keep what a line finishes

Task 001-14d made the pool's checked reduction divisible into 64-history blocks of a piece and sized lines from the
pieces' history-draws (`app/analysis/tree_pool_jobs.py`), budgeting 600 check-seconds per core at an assumed 10 ms
per history-draw (`budget = workers * 600 / .01`). Run on all of walk-g1 (GitHub run 38219597360, 12 lines of about
235,000 history-draws each; outputs in `var/remote/poolg1-parts2/out-*`: `out/command.txt`, `out/log.txt` with a line
per finished block, `var/diag/pool-*/result.json`), only two lines finished, at about 16 and 34 ms of core time per
history-draw; the other ten stopped at the 1,000-second budget with `unfinished_export` and lost everything, though
their logs show most of their blocks had finished. The 13th line, the whole-versus-ranges comparison on g1-001
(`tools/benchmark_tree_ranges.py`), also ran out of time and gave no verdict.

Two changes, so the stage fits GitHub's 20-minute lines and a slow line costs only its unfinished blocks:
- Size lines from a rate that is measured, not assumed: take it from the finished blocks' timings in these logs
  (report the distribution, by piece and by history width), choose a default with margin so lines finish in about 12
  minutes, and let the stage generator take the rate as an option so the next measurement can change it without a code
  change.
- A line that runs out of time must still write a valid partial of the blocks it finished, and say exactly which ranges
  are left, so that a follow-up stage can reduce only those. Coverage must stay exact: the combine still stops on a
  duplicate or a missing range, and a partial must never claim a block it did not finish.

Also make the whole-versus-ranges comparison fit one line (a smaller real piece, or a bounded range of g1-001), so it
gives a verdict on GitHub.

Put any trial lines in `var/diag/001-14g/run.txt`, not `run.txt` (another worker is editing that). Another worker is
also changing the walk (`app/disputes/chronological.py`) and the checks (`app/analysis/walk_checks.py`); keep to the
pool's stage generation and reduction (`tree_pool_jobs.py`, `tree_parts.py`, `tree_pool.py`) and the benchmark tool.

Done: the measured rate distribution reported; the generator takes the rate and its default projects walk-g1's lines at
about 12 minutes; a line stopped mid-way leaves a valid partial plus its remaining ranges, and combining a stopped
line's partial with a follow-up line's partial equals reducing the same ranges in one go (shown by a test); the
comparison line fits within 20 minutes.
