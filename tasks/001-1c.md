# 001-1c One rule for what the walk asks next, and a walk that costs what the distinct states cost

Three defects in one afternoon were one defect: a decision's answers were chosen from the state when the walk
visited it, before an earlier-dated event the walk visits later (a ripe-response offering before the floor, a
levy before the notes' ripe response, a day-74 set-aside before a day-77 floor). Connor's and Codex's sessions
from 1 to 5 October fixed this pair by pair for a week (`_first_distress`, `_first_listing`, `waits`/`until`,
`_next_distress`, the earlier-distress terminal check, supported-population ordering); PLAN.md on this branch has
the record. Each fix was right and the next pair still failed, because the walk is a hand-ordered program
(litigation chain, distress loop, listing chain, notes) that pulls dated decisions forward case by case.

The class fix is one rule: at every state, each chain reports its next decision and that decision's date on each
draw; the walk asks, on each draw, the earliest one; draws whose earliest decision differs go separately. Then a
decision's offered answers are taken after every event dated before it on that draw, by construction, whatever the
chains are.

The walk's cost is the same problem seen from the other side. The 6 October walk emitted 12.4M paths (5.5M after
pooling merged equivalent ones) at about 1.3-1.5 paths a second per process, about 10 hours on ~290 processes, and
choosing what to ask next replays chains (`_trace(s.steps + (c,))`) at every step. A state is what decides the
future: cash and obligations per draw, pending dated events, and the tags a later question's situation reads.
Histories that reach the same state at the same date have the same future and should be walked once. Do not accept
that the walk needs hours; find what it should cost.

Do this in two parts and stop after the first:
1. A short design note in `tasks/001-1c-design.md`: the rule, the state that decides the future, how the walk
   advances and shares states, what stays exactly as it is (the engine's cash processing and booking rules, the
   question rules in QUESTIONS_20240514.md, per-draw situation classes, every feasible stress path, probability
   composition), and what the page and reduction need from the walk if it no longer lists every history.
2. A prototype of that walk on the families that failed today (`var/diag/`, the paths in
   `tests/test_dated_answer_domains.py` and `tests/test_ripe_after_levy.py`, and the replay roots in
   `var/diag/rewalk_other.py`), checked against the current walker: zero infeasible answers, the same cash per
   draw where both are right, probabilities summing to 1 on every draw. Measure its time and the number of states
   against the current walker's paths and time on the same roots, and project the full tree.

No cloud and no judgment calls. Measurements on local roots are enough.
