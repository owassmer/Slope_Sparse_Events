# 001-1l The chronological walk starts from any state, including the review date

The chronological walk (`app/disputes/chronological.py`) always asks the earliest pending decision, and its own
invariant (`tests/walk_order.py`) shows it skips none. But it starts only from one hand-seeded frontier
(`ChronologicalWalk.run_from`: a stayed case before the post-trial ruling), so it fits 12 of the full tree's 756
native roots at the production cut (`tools/measure_chronological.py` builds the inventory; the last run's is in
`var/diag/001-1k/roots.json`). 684 roots are not stayed, and 60 need continuations it does not seed, registration
among them.

Make what is pending come from the state, not from a hand-written frontier. From any state (the review date with
nothing decided, or any native root's prefix), the walk derives each chain's next unresolved decision from what the
state holds, and walks from there by the same rule. The litigation, settlement, stay, registration, response, notes,
listing and distress chains all take part. The answers, question classes and booking rules stay as they are.

Show it on a single draw (`--draws 1` style; this Mac can't hold 512-draw walks): from the review date the walk
completes with zero skipped decisions and probabilities summing to 1; and every one of the 756 native roots starts
and walks on one draw without error, with the no-skip check on a spread of them. Put the 512-draw versions of those
runs in `run.txt`. Data goes in `var/`; what you noticed goes in your final message. No cloud and no judgment calls.
