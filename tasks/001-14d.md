# 001-14d: spread the checked reduction by work, not by artifact

The pool's reduction stage (`slope pool-tree --partial`, lines generated by `app/analysis/tree_pool_jobs.py`, work in
`app/analysis/tree_parts.py` and `tree_history_checks.py`) now runs the dated-facts and no-skip checks on every
history. Outcome 001 needs that on the whole walked tree, about 8 million histories and 100 million history-draws, in
lines of a 4-core GitHub runner limited to 20 minutes, at most 256 lines a run, 40 at once.

First real run, on walk-g1 (GitHub run 38209446710, 160 pieces, 56,463 histories, 2.70M history-draws; one heavy
artifact of four pieces per line; outputs in `var/remote/poolg1-parts/out-*`, each with `out/log.txt`,
`out/command.txt` and `var/diag/pool-*/result.json`; the pieces' manifests in `var/remote/walk-g1/out-*/`):
- 27 lines finished, 20,037 histories and 785k history-draws in 7,683 line-seconds; 13 stopped at the 1,000-second
  budget with `unfinished_export` (36,426 histories, 1.92M history-draws), and their partials are lost because a
  GitHub job's workspace does not survive to a rerun.
- The checks themselves ran at about 0.005-0.01 seconds per history-draw on the one core doing the work. At that rate
  the whole tree is roughly 300 core-hours, under two hours on 160 cores.
- But work is handed out by artifact and, within a line, by piece. One piece can carry most of a line's work (the
  heaviest held 233k of its line's 236k history-draws), so one core works while three wait, and a line holding a heavy
  piece cannot finish in 20 minutes no matter how many lines run.

Make the reduction divisible by work: a line (and each of its four workers) must be able to take a bounded range of
one piece's histories, and the stage generator must size lines by history-draws from the pieces' manifests so each
finishes in about 12 minutes using all four cores (room for slow runners and downloads). A piece split across lines or workers is still counted exactly
once: every history in exactly one range, every range in exactly one part, and the combine still stops on a duplicate
or a missing range, as it stops on a duplicate piece now. The verdicts, masses and sample must be identical whether a
piece is reduced whole or in ranges (show it by a test, and on a real heavy piece).

Separately, `tests/test_tree_pool_jobs.py::test_generated_stages_with_real_upload_layout` now fails on GitHub: the pool
command inside it exits with `IndexError('list index out of range')` (seen in run `checks-cmp14c`; it passed after
task 001-13d). Find the cause and fix it; if it shows a real fault in a stage, say so.

Real heavy pieces: the walk-g1 heavy artifacts are on GitHub, not here; `var/remote/walk-f34/heavy-0/` holds four real
pieces (one with 11,603 history-draws). Keep local runs to a few minutes; put real-size trials in `run.txt`.

Done: the generator's lines for walk-g1 are sized by history-draws and each projects within 20 minutes on 4 cores
from the measured rate; a piece reduced in ranges gives exactly what it gives whole; duplicate and missing ranges stop
the combine; the stages test passes.
