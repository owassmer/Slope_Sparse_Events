# 001-14a: question facts must not read a value sized on a later date

A question is defined by the state on its own date; later answers never define it. Task 001-13f found, in real
pieces of the current walk (walk-f34), questions whose facts carry a value computed from events after their date. That
is future-information leakage, a critical error: the judgment model would answer from facts it could not have had.
The whole walk (about 6 million histories) has stopped and will be walked again once this class is gone.

The instance found (evidence and reproduction in `var/diag/001-13f/`: `verdict.md`, `smallest-collateral.json`,
`var/cause.py`; regression fixture `tests/fixtures/walk_f34_walk-f34-0002_168_judgment_response.json` with
`tests/test_walk_f34_dated_facts.py`): the ripe judgment-response question on 20 August 2024 (day 98) shows collateral
of $2.95M where the state on that day gives $12.93M. `Chain.collateral_before` (`app/analysis/events.py`) picks the
latest stay motion before the question (filed day 95) and returns `stay_facts(i)['collateral']`, which
`_size_stay` -> `bond_collateral` -> `owed_at(approval)` sizes on the stay's later approval date (day 133), after a
payment funded by an offering that closes on day 103. In walk-f34-0002, 28 of 214 histories (1,032 history-draws) carry
it; the other three pieces none.

Fix the class, not the instance. Find every path by which a question's facts can read state that is sized, dated or
resolved after the question's own date (a stay's approval-date sizing is one; look for others of the same kind: values
memoised at a later event, "latest" selections that return a later-computed figure, settlement or offering outcomes
read before they happen). For each, make the question see what stood on its own date. Where a pending motion's
proposed terms are the right fact (the code's comment says the motion establishes proposed terms while approval is
pending), size them from what was known no later than the question's date, and say in your report which date you
chose and why, against the question rule. The cash the engine books when the stay is later approved is a separate
matter: it may rightly be sized on the approval date; do not change booking unless it is wrong too, and if it is, say
so with evidence.

Show it on real histories: the 001-13f fixture is a saved record of the old walk; leave it as it is (it must keep
failing the checker) and show that the same history walked on the fixed code records the dated value; a planted later-sized value must still
fail the dated-facts check (`app/analysis/walk_checks.py`); and a bounded walk on the fixed code (well under a few
minutes on this machine, or a `run.txt` line) must show no question whose facts differ from its before-answer state,
across the question kinds the walk reaches. Do not touch the dated-facts checker's own speed; another task owns that.

Done: every path you found is listed with its fix; the real fixture and the bounded walk check clean; the planted
case still fails.
