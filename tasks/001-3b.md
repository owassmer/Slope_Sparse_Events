# 001-3b Terms known on the decision's own date

`QUESTIONS_20240514.md` §1 (kit, Akoustis design folder): a question is defined by the state on its own date; a later
decision's answer is never part of it.

The no-future check finds every stay motion's recorded facts disagreeing with a replay of the motion-day state
(`record_replay_mismatch`; four 20-minute draws are summarised in `var/remote/001-1p/`). The security a motion offers
is sized on the court's approval day, with decisions taken between motion and approval already in the books
(`Chain._size_stay`, `_snapshot_tail`, `state14.security_offered`). The same channel prices a settlement offer at its
payment date through later answers, such as a registration order not yet made (`Chain.settle`, `seen_at`).
`var/diag/001-1m/inventory.md` has the detail.

The terms a question states are those knowable on its own date: what the company can offer as security, or the amount
it offers to settle, from the state that day plus what is already scheduled or committed, by the existing rule's
formula, never through a later answer. The court's own approval and the booking on the approval or payment day still
use that day's state. The stay sizing rule and the settlement formula stay as stated in QUESTIONS; only what they are
computed from changes.

Done when the no-future check reports no stay-motion mismatch and no settlement pricing through a later answer on one
draw for a few minutes, the no-skip check stays clean, and the stay tests pass. No cloud and no judgment calls.
