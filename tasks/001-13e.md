# 001-13e: check dated facts and no-skip on every history in the pool

Before the judgment model answers anything, outcome 001 requires the walked tree to pass, among others:
- Dated facts: every question's facts are as they stood before its own answer.
And the walk itself must ask the earliest pending decision each time, never skipping one (the question rule in
QUESTIONS_20240514.md §1, on branch `step-9-memo` in the kit's Akoustis design folder: a question is defined by the
state on its own date; later answers never define it).

The pool on GitHub's runners (`slope pool-tree`, stages from `app/analysis/tree_pool_jobs.py`; reductions in
`app/analysis/tree_parts.py`) now checks coverage, probability and offered answers over every history. Its gates file
reports `no_future` and `no_skip` only as `sample_ready_for_review` over a 32-history sample. A real run on walk-f34
(63,982 histories, 160 pieces) gave: answers pass on all histories; no_future and no_skip on 32. A future fact
reaching a question, or a skipped decision, anywhere in the other histories would pass unseen, and either one is a
critical error (future-information leakage; a question not defined by its own date). The checks already exist as code:
`tests/walk_order.py` (`Checker`, `FutureChecker`) and `tools/check_question_futures.py`, used so far on bounded walks.

Make the part reduction run both checks on every history it reduces, and make the combined gates report each as
pass or fail with counts and the first failing examples, the same way answers is reported. A failure in a part must
reach the final verdict. The sample stays, for the independent replay.

This runs inside the existing stages, so it has to fit their limits: each line a 4-core runner, at most 20 minutes;
measured reduction today is about 50-90 histories a second per line (walk-f34: 19,946 histories in 430 s). Measure what
the checks add per history; the whole reduction stage should stay within about two hours of wall time for 8 million histories at 40 lines at
once (today it projects near 80 minutes). If the checks push it past that, make them cheaper rather than sampling them. The
whole tree is expected near 8 million histories. Use `var/remote/pool-t2-*` (the walk-f34 trial outputs) and real
pieces from `var/remote/walk-f*` for measurement; put any real-size trial in `run.txt`.

Done: on real pieces, the gates report no_future and no_skip over every history; a test shows one planted future fact
and one planted skipped decision each fail the combined verdict when they sit in a part other than the first; the
measured cost per history is reported with the projected reduction time for 8 million histories.
