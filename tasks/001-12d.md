# 001-12d Keep the isolation probe whole; cut the source instead

Adding the full 19 Sep 2023 DEF 14A to the kit (`akts_2023_09_19_def14a_full`) brought in a pre-cutoff sentence that
names a 22 May 2024 shareholder-proposal deadline. It collided with an isolation probe for later judgment information,
and the probe test was narrowed to let that one occurrence through (`tests/test_evidence_snapshots.py`).

This kit's convention for that case: never weaken a probe. Cut the irrelevant section (the future-proposals section)
from the kit's copy of the source, and record the cut in the source's catalog entry (`kit_copy` in
`research/revision_v2/data/sources.json`), as the kit already does for the excerpted 2023 proxy. Restore the probe test
to its form before the narrowing. Rebuild the 14 May snapshot, refresh the manifest, and keep the evidence, isolation
and baby-shelf tests passing; the ownership table and the officers' list the baby-shelf rule cites must still be in the
kit copy. No cloud and no judgment calls.
