# 001-8a Each piece of the walk hands on what the next steps need, compactly

`slope walk-tree` walks the full tree from the review date in pieces, all 512 draws together. A first wave of 64
pieces on GitHub's runners (15 minutes each; summaries in `var/remote/walk-w1/`) walked 111,168 histories covering 3.1
million history-draws, and wrote 0.134 MB of events per history: the whole tree, about 2 million histories, would be
about 300 GB. Each history covers about 28 draws on average, yet stores rows for all 512.

Make a piece hand on what pooling, the question catalog and the reduction need, and nothing more: each history's
financial rows only for the draws it covers, its probability edges and support, and the piece's question records
deduplicated by question key; compressed. Show on one partition locally (a few minutes) that the compact output
gives exactly the same histories, per-draw cash and Slope line, probability edges and question records as today's,
and report the size per history before and after. Keep the full events format readable for comparison. Results in
`var/` and your final message. No cloud and no judgment calls.
