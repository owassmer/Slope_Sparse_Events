# 001-7c A question's facts are recorded when it is asked

`QUESTIONS_20240514.md` §1 (kit, Akoustis design folder): a question is defined by the state on its own date.

The walk now asks decisions in date order, so when it asks a question it already holds the state on that question's
date. Yet some questions are recorded late and rebuilt from the completed history at finishing time (deferred
questions: `Forecaster.record_late` and its reconstruction), which is 41% of finishing time on a heavy draw
(`var/diag/001-7a/report.md`). It also lets later steps in: on draw 0 from the review date, history 85's early
registration collateral, rebuilt at finishing, changes with a later listing answer (`var/diag/001-7b/report.md`).

In the chronological walk, record each question's key, class and facts from the state when it is asked, and stop
rebuilding them at finishing. Where a question's facts really need something not yet booked on its own date, say
what and why, rather than reading later steps. Check on the saved root and a few minutes of the review root: question
records equal today's wherever today's carried no future, history 85's record no longer moves with the listing
answer, both checks are clean, and the walk's histories and cash are unchanged. Report finishing time before and
after on the same heavy draw. No cloud and no judgment calls.
