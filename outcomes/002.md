# 002 The Akoustis 14 May page from the answer-first walk

On a fresh clone, one command runs the Akoustis 14 May case with the answer-first walk and serves its page in under an
hour on a laptop. Every collections, timing and exposure figure is a range no wider than 1% of the commitment, with the
worst feasible path beside it, and changing a judgment moves them. No question's facts can see anything after its own
date, shown by a test that changes every later answer and finds the facts unchanged.

(Owen confirmed this wording on 28 October 2026. It replaces outcome 001, whose full-tree walk and every-history audit
gave way to the answer-first walk the same day. The 1% width and the one-hour limit were the coordinator's proposal,
accepted with it. Clef runs only on Owen's start.)

Owen, 28 October, on the review's finding that a percentile cannot promise a dollar width: the 1% of the commitment
holds for expected values (collections, exposure, a month's collections, probabilities). Percentile figures (P5, P95,
the P5 of headroom) hold to a probability tolerance (P5 within the levels the unexpanded mass allows, for example
P4.5 to P5.5), with the dollar range that implies shown as it is.

Owen, 28 October, after the prototype showed the unwalked tail shrinking only by half for each tenfold more walking:
the likely outcomes are walked exactly and the rest is sampled from the judge's own answers, so each range is a 99%
interval from that sampling (labelled as such), within 1% of the commitment; the worst feasible path stays a separate
search.
