---
name: grill
description: Question the person until the purpose or an outcome is in their own words. Use before writing either.
---
Ask one question at a time. Frame each as two to four concrete options, and state your lean and why in one
line. The person may pick one, combine them, or answer in their own words.
Cover: who the user is; what they can do afterwards that they could not before; what must stay as it is;
what done looks like in the running product.
Follow the words the person reaches for and ask what they mean by them. Where an answer contradicts something
written earlier, ask which one holds.
Stop when the person can state it in one short paragraph without you. Write that paragraph in their words and
nothing else.
